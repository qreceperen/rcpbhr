# Little Classroom

**Date started:** November 2026
**Category:** Teaching

## 💡 The idea
A classroom game where I am the teacher and my students (5–6 years old) answer questions and earn stars.

## 🛠️ What we built
- **13 lessons:** Counting, Letters, Colors, Shapes, Turkish, Animals 🐮, Opposites ↕️, Bigger or Smaller 🐘🐭, What Comes Next? 🔁, Easy Math ➕, Feelings 😊, Telling Time 🕒, and **my own questions**
- **4 games:**
  - 🌊 Land or Sea? Drag the animals to where they live
  - 🃏 Matching Pairs: turn over cards to find two that match
  - ✏️ Trace the Letter: follow the big letter with your finger
  - 🫧 Recess Time: pop bubbles for 30 seconds, then back to class!
- **My teacher's tools:**
  - 🧒 My students: type their names, they take turns ("Ali, your turn!"), and everyone earns their own stars
  - 🎤 My voice: record myself saying "Good job!", "Try again!" and "Great job, class!" and the game plays MY voice
  - 🖍️ Chalkboard: draw and write with colored chalk, an eraser and a "clean board" button
  - 🫙 Class jar: every star drops a marble in the jar. 20 marbles = CLASS PARTY! 🎉
- The game reads every question out loud (little kids can't read yet!)
- Stars for right answers on the first try, plus a ⭐ Teacher's star button
- A surprise sticker at the end of every lesson

## 🔒 Private things
Student names and my voice recordings stay only on this computer. They never go on the website.

## ✏️ How to write my own questions
Open `lessons.js`, copy a question block, and change the words. Save, then refresh the page.

## 🎨 Easy things to change
- `jar.js`: `JAR_SIZE` is how many marbles fill the jar
- `recess.js`: `RECESS_SECONDS` is how long recess lasts
- `sorting.js`: the animals in Land or Sea
- `matching.js`: the pictures on the cards
- `more-lessons.js`: the animals, opposites, feelings and patterns
