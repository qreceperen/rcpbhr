# Bedroom Escape

**Date started:** November 2026
**Category:** Puzzle

## 💡 The idea
A room escape game! You wake up locked in your bedroom. Find clues, solve math puzzles, and get out.

## 🛠️ What we built
- A bedroom with a bed, bookshelf, desk, wardrobe, toy box, rug, poster and a locked door
- 🎒 A backpack: click an item, then click where to use it
- ✨ Found things glow INSIDE where they are (in the toy box, drawer, safe...). Click them and they fly into the backpack
- 🔦 Real light: it's dark under the bed until the flashlight beam shines there
- ✋ The item you're holding follows your mouse
- Put two items together by clicking one, then the other: they spin together into something new ✨
- 🔢 Number locks with a keypad (you can type on the keyboard too!)
- 🧮 Math puzzles: a number pattern, math on the books, and picture math for the safe (🧸 + 🧸 = 10...)
- ✨ Fairy lights, a globe, one cat on the window and a teddy on the bed
- 💡 A hint button and ⏱️ a timer: try to escape fast with no hints!
- Same style as the Cafeteria: a big start screen, fast-growing buttons, and X starts the game

## 🤫 The solution
It's all written inside `game.js` (but no peeking before you escape!)

## 🎨 Easy things to change in `game.js`
- `LOCKS`: the lock codes and the clues on them
- `BOOKS`: the math problems on the books
- `NOTES`: what the secret notes say
- `HINTS`: the hints

⚠️ If you change a math problem, remember to change the lock code too!
