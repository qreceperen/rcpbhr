# Guess the Number

**Date started:** November 2026
**Category:** Game

## 💡 The idea
The computer picks a secret number and we try to find it with hints.

## 🛠️ What we built
A game that says "Higher!" or "Lower!" and counts our tries.

## 🧠 What we learned
- Computers can pick **random** numbers
- `if` / `else` lets the computer make decisions
- A clever trick: always guess the **middle** number, so you never need more than 7 tries!
