# Picture Puzzle

**Date started:** November 2026
**Category:** Puzzle (for the junior class)

## 💡 The idea
Drag and drop the pieces to complete a picture.

## 🛠️ What we built
- 4 pictures drawn with code: Farm, Ocean, Space, Rainbow Garden
- 4, 6, 9, 15 or 20 pieces with real jigsaw shapes (bumps and dents!)
- 👻 Helper button to show a faint picture on the board
- 📷 Use your own picture: take a photo of a drawing and make it a puzzle!
- Works with a mouse AND fingers on a tablet

## 🎨 How to add a new picture
Open `pictures.js`, copy one picture block, and draw something new with
`emoji(...)`, `circle(...)` and colors.
