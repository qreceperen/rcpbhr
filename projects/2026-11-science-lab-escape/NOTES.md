# Science Lab Escape

**Date started:** November 2026
**Category:** Puzzle / Science

## 💡 The idea
Another room escape game! This time you are locked in a science lab, and every puzzle is a real science experiment, like mixing things.

## 🛠️ What we built
- A science lab with a lab coat, a potion cabinet, an elements poster, a fish tank, a lab bench, a freezer and a lab door with a number lock
- 🥽 Safety first! You have to wear goggles before you can mix potions
- 🎨 A potion lock: mix two colors in a beaker to fill each tube (🍇 🥕 🐸)
- 🧊 Melt ice on a hot plate to find what's frozen inside
- 🌋 A volcano that ERUPTS with baking soda + vinegar
- 🧲 A magnet that pulls a metal tin out of the fish tank
- 🍋 Secret lemon-juice ink that shows up near a warm lamp
- 🧪 A real elements poster: the door code is hiding in it!
- 🔬 A science fact pops up every time you do an experiment
- 💡 Hints and ⏱️ a timer, just like Bedroom Escape
- Same style as our other games: big start screen, fast-growing buttons, and X starts the game

## 🤫 The solution
It's all written inside `game.js` (but no peeking before you escape!)

## 🎨 Easy things to change in `game.js`
- `POTION_LOCK`: the pictures on the tubes and the colors they want
- `LOCKS`: the door code
- `NOTES`: what the secret note says
- `HINTS`: the hints

⚠️ If you change the letters on the secret note, remember to change the door code too!
